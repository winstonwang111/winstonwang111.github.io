Professional Summary / 个人简介

Doctoral researcher working at the intersection of nonlinear dynamics, conservative chaos, energy-based analysis, and intelligent systems. Research interests include high-dimensional conservative chaotic systems, reinforcement learning, and chaotic phenomena in the neural networks underlying large language models. Professional experience includes banking risk assessment, robotic process automation, and satellite-imagery-based post-loan monitoring.

从事非线性动力学、保守混沌、能量分析与智能系统交叉研究的博士研究生。 研究兴趣包括高维保守混沌系统、强化学习，以及大语言模型所使用的神经网络中的混沌现象。 工作经历涉及银行风险评估、机器人流程自动化和卫星影像辅助贷后监测。
